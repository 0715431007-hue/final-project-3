Deltarune is an episodic RPG by Toby Fox set in a world where human Kris, monster Susie, and Dark Prince Ralsei form a party to seal "Dark Fountains" threatening their world. Kris (the one the player controls) has to make decisions that impact the gameplay.

Deltarune follows Kris, a human teenager, who teams up with monster classmate Susie and the charming Ralsei, a prince from the Dark World, to fulfill a prophecy and seal powerful Dark Fountains that threaten their world, exploring themes of destiny, friendship, and escapism through fantastical adventures in unique worlds.
