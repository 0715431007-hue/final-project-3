Undertale is a 2015 indie RPG game by Toby Fox, where a human child falls into a creature-filled underground and must find their way out. The child must make decisions on what they should do. These decisions alter the route and ending of the game.

A human child who has fallen into the Underground: a large place under the surface of the Earth, separated by a magical barrier. The child meets many monsters during the journey back to the surface, and has to make decisions which impact the games' ending.
